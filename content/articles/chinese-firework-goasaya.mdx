## A Chinese New Year Dessert Inspired by Celebration

Named Chinese Firework, this dessert draws inspiration from the energy and symbolism of traditional New Year fireworks. Not the noise or brightness, but the moment they represent. A wish for renewal, good fortune, and shared beginnings.

Rather than translating this idea literally, GoaSaya approaches it through texture, flavor, and structure. The dessert is composed with restraint, allowing each element to unfold gradually.

### Chinese Firework

At its core, Chinese Firework is a milk chocolate entremets that feels smooth and composed. Beneath the surface sits a ginger crèmeux, bringing warmth and gentle spice. A sparkling orange core introduces brightness and movement, echoing the celebratory spirit of the season.

The layers are built with almond jaconde and sablé, offering softness and subtle crunch. Mandarin segment jelly adds freshness, while a sesame tuile provides contrast and a final textural accent. Together, these elements create a dessert that feels dynamic yet balanced.

The combination symbolizes luck and prosperity for the New Year. Sweetness is present, but never overwhelming. Each bite reflects intention rather than excess.
