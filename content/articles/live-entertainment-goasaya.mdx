![Jazz band playing at Goasaya PIK 2](/articles/entertainment/1.webp)

There is something deeply captivating about acoustic music echoing through our stone tunnel corridors and textured walls. The canyon-like design of Goasaya naturally enhances the acoustics of the room, creating an intimate, warm soundscape.

## A Visual Spectacle: The Mesmerizing Tanoura Dance

![Tanoura dancer performing at Goasaya Altar](/articles/entertainment/2.webp)

For guests seeking a truly unique visual experience, Goasaya regularly hosts traditional Tanoura dance performances. Originating from the Middle East, this captivating, spinning dance perfectly complements the mystical, ceremonial energy of our central Altar. 

As the dancer spins, the illuminated, colorful skirts create a mesmerizing optical illusion that lights up the cave-inspired interior. It is a stunning, high-energy performance that pairs beautifully with the dramatic aesthetic of the restaurant, offering a cultural and visual spectacle rarely found in Jakarta's dining scene.
